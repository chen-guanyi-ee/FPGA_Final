Final functional fixes
----------------------
- line_setup: changed the horizontal ray-origin offset from the old 800-wide
  centre (400 pixels) to the XGA centre (512 pixels). camera_proj already used
  512, so the stale constant shifted the table/rails and all world-space lines
  by 112 pixels relative to the projected balls, making prediction lines look
  detached and balls appear to enter the cushion.
- camera_proj/gen_roms: widened low-view vertical projection fields from signed
  14-bit to signed 15-bit. The old cue-ball value 8,901 wrapped negative, so the
  center-view ball vanished while the top view and minimap remained correct.
- top_nexys: set UART_CLKS_PER_BIT=564 for 65 MHz / 115200 baud. The old value
  347 belonged to the former 40 MHz clock and caused COM5 packets to be ignored.
- game_ctrl: reset now waits for physics busy to deassert after BRAM
  initialization rather than waiting only two clocks.
- top_nexys: removed/tied off the board LED and seven-segment status display
  path while retaining the VGA renderer.
- Ball physics state remains stored in two true-dual-port BRAM banks; simulation
  confirms GAME/PRED separation and exact physics behavior.

---------------------------------------
1. Check build/timing.rpt for WNS and hold slack.
2. Check build/util.rpt and build/util_hier.rpt for slice/LUT growth.
3. Check build/power.rpt and its confidence level.
4. Confirm report timestamps are newer than the XGA run start.
5. Do not generate/program a bitstream until timing and DRC are acceptable.

Rejected or deferred ideas
--------------------------
- Two cycles per pixel / updating every second frame: rejected for direct VGA
  scanout. VGA still needs one pixel every 65 MHz clock. This only works with a
  full framebuffer and a separate scanout domain.
- Halving frame rate for resolution: rejected. Non-standard low-refresh VGA
  reduces compatibility and does not remove the one-pixel-per-clock scanout.
- Merging GAME and PRED banks: rejected; prediction would corrupt/stall live
  physics state.
- Merging camera work and commit banks: rejected; partial updates can tear.
- Moving the small active slot array to BRAM: rejected for now. Many fields are
  needed in parallel per pixel, requiring BRAM replication or serialization.
- Forcing large slot structures into LUTRAM: deferred; asynchronous parallel
  reads and routing may worsen timing.
- Unsafe multicycle constraints for 65 MHz: rejected. Constraints must reflect
  real latency, not hide failing paths.
- Two renderer copies or multisample antialiasing: rejected because it multiplies
  LUT/DSP cost and no meaningful aliasing problem was visible.

Recommended next step if 65 MHz timing fails
--------------------------------------------
- Do not relax timing artificially.
- Split into a 65 MHz VGA/render domain and a 40 MHz game/physics domain.
- Transfer stable frame-level state through a proper snapshot/CDC scheme.
- Re-run implementation and compare against the archived 800x600 baseline.

Possible low-risk follow-up
---------------------------
- rom_sync forces block RAM. Tiny 64x8 rom_lx/rom_ly tables could use selectable
  distributed ROM to avoid full RAMB18 use. BRAM is not the main limit.
- Three gamma ROM copies are intentional for simultaneous RGB addresses.
